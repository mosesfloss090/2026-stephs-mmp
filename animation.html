Making animation is fun to me because it feels like giving life to ideas. A character, drawing, or object starts completely still, but frame by frame it begins to move, express emotion, and tell a story. Watching something come alive because of your own creativity is an amazing feeling. I enjoy how much freedom animation gives me. I can create worlds, movements, and scenes that would be impossible in real life. Whether it’s smooth action, funny moments, or dramatic effects, every movement adds personality and energy.
